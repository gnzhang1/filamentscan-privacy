Privacy Policy — Filament Scan

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Filament Scan is a fully offline filament inventory app. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

None. Filament Scan does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no user account, no analytics, no advertising, and no network communication — the app holds no INTERNET permission.

Permissions and on-device use

  • Camera — used only to scan barcodes on filament spools. Camera frames are processed on your device in real time for barcode decoding; no images are saved or sent anywhere.
  • NFC — used only to read NFC tags on filament spools.
  • Vibrate — haptic feedback when a scan succeeds.

Data stored on your device

Your inventory (filament types, colours, quantities, notes) is stored in a local database on your device only. Backup files, CSV exports, and shared stock lists are created only when you explicitly choose to create them, and are saved wherever you choose.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes the local database and all inventory data from your device. Files you exported or backed up yourself (e.g. to Downloads or cloud storage) must be deleted by you at their location.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
